Section 2

How to Delete Your Account & Data (Step-by-Step)

1 In-App Account & Cloud Backup Deletion (If Signed In)

If you used optional Sign in with Google to back up your launcher data to Google Drive:

  1. Open Sukun on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm the prompt.
This deletes your cloud backup in your Google Drive app-data folder (when deletion succeeds), removes account details from the device, and signs you out.

2 Complete Local Wipe via Android Settings (All Users)

To completely and immediately erase all local launcher configurations, notes, tasks, reminders, cached weather, and prayer logs from your device:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps → Sukun.
  3. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage / Clear data and confirm.

3 Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling Sukun immediately removes the application and deletes its private sandbox directory from your Android device.

  1. Long-press the Sukun app icon.
  2. Tap Uninstall (or drag to the uninstall trash bin).
  3. Confirm uninstallation to remove all application data.

4 In-App Deletion of Specific Content

You can also selectively delete content within the launcher:

  • Notes & Tasks: Delete individual notes or todo items.
  • Reminders: Delete specific scheduled reminders in the reminders screen.
  • Prayer Logs: Delete logs manually or let them expire after the 2-year retention window.
  • Wallpapers & Audio: Reset customized azan or chime choices in settings.
