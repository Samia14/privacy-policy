Section 2

How to Delete Your Data (Step-by-Step)

You can delete any or all data stored by Sukun using any of the following methods:

1 Complete Data Wipe via Android Settings (Recommended)

To completely and immediately erase all launcher configurations, notes, tasks, reminders, cached weather, and prayer logs from your device:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or Apps & notifications / Manage Apps).
  3. Select Sukun from the list of applications.
  4. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  5. Tap Clear storage / Clear data and confirm.
This immediately purges all local databases, preferences, and cached files created by Sukun.

2 Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling Sukun immediately removes the application and deletes its private app storage directory from your Android device.

  1. Locate the Sukun app icon or find it in your device settings.
  2. Long-press the icon and tap Uninstall (or drag to the uninstall trash bin).
  3. Confirm uninstallation to remove all application data.

3 In-App Deletion of Specific Content

If you wish to delete specific items without wiping all settings:

  • Notes & Tasks: Swipe or tap delete on any individual note or task within the productivity screens.
  • Reminders: Open reminders list and delete scheduled reminder entries.
  • Custom Wallpapers & Sounds: Reset wallpaper settings or revert custom azan / chime audio selections in the app settings menu.
  • Prayer Logs: Delete logs directly or wait for the automatic 2-year expiration cycle.

Section 4

Third-Party Services Data Handling

Sukun does not host an intermediate backend. If you enable optional features, your device communicates directly with third-party providers via encrypted HTTPS:

  • Open-Meteo (Weather): Coordinates or city names sent for weather lookup are processed in real-time and not linked to any user profile.
  • Unsplash (Daily Wallpaper): Wallpaper requests are standard anonymous HTTP image downloads.
  • Search Providers (DuckDuckGo / Google): Search queries triggered by the user in the app drawer are handled directly by the selected search engine under their respective privacy policies.

No user identifiers, telemetry, or analytics profiles are shared with third parties.
