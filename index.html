Section 2

2. Summary

  • Sukun works without signing in. Sign-in is optional and used for backup/sync.
  • Launcher settings, notes, todos, reminders, prayer logs, and usage summaries are stored locally on your device unless you choose to back them up.
  • Google Sign-In (OAuth) lets you sync a backup to your Google Drive app-data folder.
  • Location is used only if you enable optional weather or prayer-time features.
  • Usage access is used only if you enable optional screen time or app cooldown features.
  • Accessibility is optional — used for double-tap lock, home-button recents, and Focus mode.
  • Network requests occur for optional features you enable, when you sign in/sync, for Premium purchases, and when you open external links or search.

3. Information the app accesses and collects

3.1 Data we do not collect on our own servers

We do not operate servers that receive or store:

  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, videos, or general file contents
  • Payment card or bank details (handled by Google Play)
  • Browsing history (except when you deliberately open a browser or search engine)

We do not sell your personal information.

3.3 Optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup

If you choose Sign in with Google:

  • Sukun receives your Google account ID, name, email, and profile photo through Google’s OAuth sign-in flow.
  • These details are stored locally on your device to show your account in Settings.
  • With your permission, Sukun may upload a backup file to the app-data folder of your Google Drive account. This backup may include settings, home apps, reminders, todos, prayer rollups, and screen-time rollups — not Premium purchase status.
  • Sukun does not receive your Google password.
  • You can Sign out at any time in Settings → Account.
  • You can Delete account & data in Settings → Account to delete the cloud backup and remove account details from the device (see Section 7 & Section 8).
  • Google processes sign-in and Drive storage under Google’s Privacy Policy.

3.4 Location data

Sukun requests location permission only for optional weather and prayer-time features.

ModeWhat happens
Manual locationYou type a city or place. Sukun may send that text to Open-Meteo’s geocoding API. GPS is not required.
Device locationWith permission, Sukun reads location to calculate prayer times or fetch weather. Data may be sent to Open-Meteo for weather lookup.
Google weather sourceSukun opens Google Search in your browser; no weather API call is made by Sukun.
Feature offIf weather and prayer times are disabled, Sukun does not access location.

We do not track your location over time or build a location history on our servers.

3.5 App activity and usage data

If you grant Usage access, Sukun reads Android usage statistics on your device only for screen time display and app cooldown rules you configure. This data is not uploaded to us.

As a launcher, Sukun reads installed applications locally for the app drawer and home screen. This list is not sent to us.

3.6 Accessibility data

If you enable Sukun’s optional accessibility service, the app may observe accessibility events to lock the screen on double tap, open recent apps from the home button, and enforce Focus mode by detecting the foreground app package name.

Accessibility data is processed only on your device. Sukun does not send accessibility events, screen content, or window text to us or third parties.

3.7 Firebase Analytics

Sukun includes Google Firebase Analytics. Firebase may automatically collect limited information such as app interactions, crash-related diagnostics, and device or app identifiers (for example, Firebase installation ID) to help us understand app usage and improve stability.

We do not use Firebase to collect your name, email, or prayer/launcher content. Firebase data is processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy.

3.8 Google Play Billing (Premium)

If you purchase Sukun Premium, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that Premium is unlocked on your device. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Google Play account and remains on each device separately; it is not included in backup files.

Section 4

4. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • Operate the launcher and optional features you enable
  • Save your preferences and content (notes, todos, reminders)
  • Calculate and display optional prayer times and weather
  • Send local notifications for reminders, prayer times, and optional hourly chime
  • Enforce optional Focus mode, Mindful Morning, and app cooldown rules
  • Sync optional backups to your Google Drive when you sign in
  • Process Premium purchases through Google Play
  • Understand app usage and stability through Firebase Analytics

We do not use your data for advertising or selling to data brokers.

Section 5

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may leave your device only when:

  1. You use optional sign-in/backup — data is stored in your Google Drive app-data folder
  2. You purchase Premium — Google Play processes the transaction
  3. Firebase Analytics — Google receives limited usage/diagnostic data
  4. Third-party services you use — weather APIs, wallpaper downloads, search engines, affiliate links (Section 3.9)
  5. Actions you initiate — e.g. sharing the app via Android’s share sheet
  6. Android backup — if enabled on your device, some app data may be included in Google/Android backup (Section 9)

How to delete your data

On device (all users):

  • Remove individual content inside the app (reminders, notes, todos, prayer logs where supported)
  • Android Settings → Apps → Sukun → Storage → Clear storage
  • Uninstall Sukun

If you signed in with Google:

  1. Open Sukun → Settings → Account
  2. Tap Delete account & data
  3. Confirm — this deletes your Google Drive backup (when successful), removes account details from the device, and signs you out

Your local settings remain after Delete account & data unless you also clear app storage or uninstall.

To remove local data completely: clear app storage or uninstall after deleting account data.

If cloud deletion fails (e.g. no internet), try again when online or contact us (Section 15).

Section 8

8. Account and data deletion

Sukun Minimalistic Launcher — Delete your account & data

Sukun does not create a separate Sukun username/password. Optional Sign in with Google is used only for backup and sync.

Steps to delete account and cloud data:

  1. Open Sukun → Settings → Account
  2. Tap Delete account & data
  3. Confirm the prompt

What is deleted:

  • Google Sign-In session in the app
  • Account name, email, and photo stored in the app
  • Cloud backup in your Google Drive app-data folder (when deletion succeeds)

What is not deleted:

  • Your Google account itself (managed by Google)
  • Sukun Premium purchase (managed by Google Play)
  • Local launcher data (until you clear storage or uninstall)

Section 9

9. Backup and device transfer

Sukun supports manual Export / Import backup files and optional Google Drive sync when signed in.

Android may also include some app data in Google cloud backup or device-to-device transfer depending on your device settings. Review Settings → System → Backup on your device.

Premium purchase status is managed by Google Play, not by backup files.

Sukun processes sensitive launcher and optional wellbeing data on your device. Network requests to Google, Firebase, Open-Meteo, and other services use HTTPS where supported.

Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android updated.
